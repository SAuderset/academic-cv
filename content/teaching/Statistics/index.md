**Description:**
This class provides an introduction to data exploration and descriptive and inferential statistics with a focus on applications in (comparative) linguistics using the programming language R. Topics include: frequentist versus Bayesian statistics; linear, logistic, and multilevel models; clustering and random forests; and other statistical analyses depending on the participants’ interests.

* level: MA and advanced BA
* taught at: University of Bern and University of California, Santa Barbara
* in: Fall 2024, Fall 2018
