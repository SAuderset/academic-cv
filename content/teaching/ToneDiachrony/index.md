title: The Diachrony of Tone

**Description:**
This seminar explores diachronic aspects of tone, the use of pitch to distinguish meaning
and grammatical categories. We will cover the emergence (tonogenesis), change, and loss
(tonoexodus) of tone in tonal languages across the world. We will discuss methodological
challenges tone poses for comparative linguistics, and how approaching these challenges
advances linguistic methodology more broadly.

* level: MA and upper level BA
* taught at: University of Bern
* in: Fall 2026
